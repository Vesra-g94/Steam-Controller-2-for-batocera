ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT · v0.8.0

Steam Controller 2 for Batocera

A userspace Bluetooth bridge that gives the Steam Controller 2 full virtual gamepad support in Batocera without rebuilding or patching the kernel.

v0.8.0 adds a dedicated virtual mouse: the right trackpad controls pointer movement, while physical clicks on the left or right half of the left trackpad act as left and right mouse buttons. Standard controls, extra buttons, rumble, and automatic Bluetooth reconnect remain intact.

Bluetooth HID 28DE:1303 has been independently confirmed on two separate Steam Controller 2 units, and normal controller operation has now passed testing on a second controller.
